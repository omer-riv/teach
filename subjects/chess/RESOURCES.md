# Chess Openings Resources

## Knowledge

- [Chess.com: "Italian Game"](https://www.chess.com/openings/Italian-Game)
  Overview of the Italian, its main variations (Giuoco Piano, Pianissimo, Evans) and ideas. Use for: anything about the Italian with White.
- [Wikibooks: Chess Opening Theory, 3. Bc4](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Chess_Opening_Theory/1._e4/1...e5/2._Nf3/2...Nc6/3._Bc4)
  Move-by-move tree with explanations; names the Two Knights and Pianissimo. Use for: checking a line and Black's alternatives.
- [Chess.com: "Chess notation"](https://www.chess.com/terms/chess-notation)
  How algebraic notation works. Use for: notation questions.
- [Lichess: "The 5 opening principles every club player needs to master"](https://lichess.org/@/Olaffo/blog/the-5-opening-principles-every-club-player-needs-to-master/uD07rPys)
  Community blog on centre, development, castling. Use for: principles when an opponent leaves the book.
- [Lichess opening practice](https://lichess.org/practice) and [Lichess puzzles by opening](https://lichess.org/training/openings)
  Free drills. Use for: extra practice between lessons.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/chessbeginners](https://www.reddit.com/r/chessbeginners/)
  Large, beginner-friendly subreddit. Use for: posting a game and asking what went wrong.
- Lichess and chess.com game analysis: review your own games after playing friends.

## Gaps
