The Italian setup

White's first six moves, and the one rule about their order.

The moves

  1. e4: take the centre.
  2. Nf3: knight out, attacks e5.
  3. Bc4: bishop aims at f7. This is the Italian Game.
  4. c3: prepares d4 later; bishop can retreat to c2.
  5. d3: defends e4.
  6. O-O: castle. King safe, rook in play.

Moves 1–3 in order. Then c3, d3, O-O in any order.
Black knight on f6? d3 first.

The main line

1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4 Bc5 4. c3 Nf6 5. d3 d6 6. O-O O-O
