Early attacks

Stopping Scholar's mate as Black.

Queen hits f7: block it, and attack the queen if you can.

f7 (f2 for White) is weak at the start because only the king defends it.

The three versions

White playsYou play
1. e4 e5 2. Bc4 Nc6 3. Qh53… g6, then if 4. Qf3, 4… Nf6
1. e4 e5 2. Qh52… Nc6 (not g6!), then after 3. Bc4, 3… g6 and 4. Qf3 Nf6
1. e4 e5 2. Bc4 Nc6 3. Qf33… Nf6

The mistakes

  • 3… Nf6?? after Qh5 (with Bc4): Qxf7#. Attacking the queen doesn't stop mate.
  • 2… g6?? after an early 2. Qh5: Qxe5+ forks king and rook h8.
  • Qe7 holds f7, but blocks your bishop. Prefer g6 or Nf6.
