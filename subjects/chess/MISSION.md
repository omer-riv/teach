# Mission: Chess Openings

## Why
Stop losing early in casual games against friends and family, by having a solid, simple plan for the first moves instead of improvising.

## Success looks like
- Play the Italian setup as White from memory and get a safe, castled king by move 6
- Handle the common things friends play instead of the main line, without panicking
- Have a simple reply as Black to 1.e4 (and later 1.d4)
- Not lose to early traps such as the four-move "Scholar's mate"

## Constraints
- Casual player: knows the rules, no opening knowledge, no real rating
- Plays on a phone app (chess.com or lichess); studies on a phone in short sessions

## Out of scope
- Deep opening theory and memorising long sharp lines (Fried Liver, gambits)
- Tournament play and rating chasing
