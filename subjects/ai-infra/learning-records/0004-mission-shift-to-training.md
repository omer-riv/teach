# Mission shifted from AI infra to training models

After lessons 1–2 (serving memory), the learner asked to focus on actually training models: what goes into it, the different ways to do it, and what to take into account, with infra on the side. They want to be able to train models themselves (occasional Colab sessions) and are now unsure whether the end goal is an infra SWE role or something closer to ML. See [[MISSION.md]].

**Implications:** Teach the training loop from first principles (light maths with worked numbers is now in scope; derivations still out). Lessons 1–2 stay valid and feed the later "training memory" lesson. Hands-on Colab lessons need a laptop, so flag them in advance.
