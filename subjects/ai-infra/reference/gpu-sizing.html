Does it fit?

  1. Weights = params × bytes.
  2. Minimum GPUs = ⌈weights ÷ GPU memory⌉. In practice round up to 2, 4 or 8.
  3. Whatever is left over is the KV cache budget: it sets how many users and how much context you can serve. Llama 3 70B: ~160 kB per token in int8, ~5.3 GB per 32k-token sequence.
